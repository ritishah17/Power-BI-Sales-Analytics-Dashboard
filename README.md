# Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive, four-page Power BI dashboard created to analyse sales performance, geographic trends, customer segments, and product performance.

## Dashboard Pages
- **Sales Performance:** Overview of sales, profit, quantity, and order trends.
- **Geographic Analysis:** Comparison of sales across geographic regions.
- **Customer Analysis:** Exploration of customer segments and regional patterns.
- **Product Performance:** Analysis of product categories, sub-categories, sales, and profit.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data visualisation
- Business and sales analytics
